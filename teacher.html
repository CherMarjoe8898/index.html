Guidelines & Help

Quick reminders for submitting and tracking lesson plans.

1. Complete your information

Enter your full name and a valid email address. Select the correct department: TLE or TechPro.

2. Select grade and term

Choose the correct grade level and Term 1, Term 2, or Term 3 before continuing.

3. Select the assigned reviewer

Choose the Master Teacher assigned to your lesson plan. Each reviewer should only access submissions assigned to their account.

4. Upload a supported lesson plan file

Accepted formats are DOC, DOCX, ODT, RTF, and PDF. Word, ODT, and RTF files are converted to Google Docs. PDFs remain PDFs for preview.

5. Keep your Submission ID

After successful submission, copy or save the Submission ID. You will need it to check the status and feedback later.

6. Understand the review status

Pending Review means awaiting review. Return to Sender means corrections are required. Approved means the Master Teacher has approved the plan.
